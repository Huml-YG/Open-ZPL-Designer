QR code

Content

Module = dots per QR square. Whole dots keep it crisp and scannable; 4+ recommended.

Barcode

Content

EAN-13: 12 digits (check digit is added) or 13 (check digit is verified). Code 128: any text, e.g. a MAC or serial number. Module = dots per bar unit; 4 ≈ standard EAN size, keep it at 2 or more. Add the digits under the bars as a text box.
